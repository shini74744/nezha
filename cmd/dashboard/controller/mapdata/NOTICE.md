# Cable map data

The marine layer and the GCM overland segments are a selection from Greg Mahlknecht’s cable map, generalised for this illustration from the 3 August 2015 Global GCM Cables layer. The published source dataset is licensed under GNU GPL version 3. The generated map contains historical routes and does not report current service or traffic. [Source layer](https://services1.arcgis.com/cc7nIINtrZ67dyVJ/ArcGIS/rest/services/Communication_Media_Use/FeatureServer/7) · [Dataset catalogue](https://api.old.datahub.io/%40global/undersea-cables/) · [GPL version 3](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html)

Canadian terrestrial routes are generalised from the Government of the Northwest Territories’ NWT Fibre Optic Lines. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Northwest Territories. [Source layer](https://www.apps.geomatics.gov.nt.ca/arcgis/rest/services/GNWT/UtilitiesCommunication_LCC/MapServer/18) · [Spatial data terms](https://www.geomatics.gov.nt.ca/en/services/spatial-data)

The land outlines, Chinese place names and label coordinates come from the project's existing Natural Earth dataset. DDnezha selects visible labels at each globe orientation. [Natural Earth terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)

Route labels identify land near the public route geometry. The generator samples the route and finds land it crosses or approaches within about 72 km. These labels do not certify individual landing stations.

## Milky Way background

The cropped and toned panorama is a photograph by ESO/S. Brunier. The background generator separates its bright stellar cores from the diffuse sky and records their image coordinates, intensities and colours. The renderer assigns illustrative distances for parallax. [Original photograph](https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
