# Carrier catalog and custom routes
Catalog refreshed 2026-09-27: 125 curated logo entries plus 1,926 country-scoped reference entries.
250 country/region options (ISO dataset plus XK); directory entries currently cover 226 regions, plus INT.
This is NOT every currently operating telecom company or every logo in the world.
The reference list may contain historical operators, private networks and MVNOs. A listing is not evidence that a service is currently sold.
## Sources and attribution
Mobile reference facts: https://mcc-mnc.org/downloads/networks.json (2,670 network records; source updated 2026-09-21).
Source license: free to use with attribution to https://mcc-mnc.org.
Grouped by country and normalized legal operator; network codes and brands retained as search aliases.
Three explicitly unknown/reserved names were excluded; exact curated country/name matches were consolidated.
carrier-directory.json stores stable IDs, labels, region, aliases and record source links; no guessed logos.
carrier-coverage.tsv records logo/reference counts by region.
Logo page and exact asset URL recorded per curated entry in carriers.ts.
HGC, HKBN, csl., 3 Hong Kong, HE, Colt and Zayo assets were obtained from their own sites.
Hong Kong operator cross-check: https://www.ofca.gov.hk/en/consumer_focus/operators_information/telecommunications_services_providers/index.html.
Additional logos: https://companieslogo.com/logos/telecommunication/ ; logos remain their owners' property, used for identification without endorsement.
No external logo service is requested for bundled entries. Missing logos stay blank and are explicitly identified in the editor.
User-supplied HTTPS logos are fetched by the visitor browser with no-referrer; small raster uploads are embedded in public notes.
Country/region options use i18n-iso-countries (MIT) and Intl.DisplayNames; INT includes global networks.
Brand entries are grouped by home market; this does not claim retail availability in all countries.
## Compatibility
Legacy networkRoute and networkRoutes.other remain readable; edits preserve old route text and unknown fields.
Custom entries store carrier, country, text and optional name/logo. Existing IDs were preserved.
Reference entries support optional user-supplied logos without changing their directory ID.
Public notes are public: do not insert private URLs, tokens or secrets.
Chinese raw labels: 其他运营商线路 / 国家地区 / 运营商 / 运营商名称 / Logo地址 / 线路名称.
Link tags: planDataMod.linkTags = [{name,url}], Chinese 链接标签 / 名称 / 网址.
Links allow HTTP(S) without credentials, open with noopener noreferrer and stop parent-card navigation.
