# Carrier catalog and custom routes
Catalog refreshed 2026-09-27: 137 curated logo entries plus 1,926 country-scoped reference entries.
250 country/region options (ISO dataset plus XK); directory entries cover 231 regions, plus INT.
This is NOT every currently operating telecom company or every logo in the world.
Reference records can include historical operators, private networks and MVNOs.

## Compatibility and customization
Legacy route text, stable carrier IDs and unknown note fields are preserved.
Custom entries: carrier, country, text, optional name/logo/color.
planDataMod.networkRouteColors configures telecom/mobile/unicom/other defaults; entry.color overrides other.
Colors accept six-digit hex only; text automatically uses black or white for contrast.
Chinese raw labels include 运营商颜色 and 标签颜色, alongside existing route labels.
PNG/JPEG/WebP/GIF upload byte and encoded-length limits were removed; browser/proxy resource limits still apply.
Public-note embedded images retain fixed 16x12 CSS-pixel frontend sizing with object-fit contain.
User-supplied HTTPS images are requested with no-referrer; invalid/executable schemes are rejected.
Public notes are public: do not insert private URLs, tokens or secrets.
Link tags retain HTTP(S) validation, noopener noreferrer, and stop card navigation.
